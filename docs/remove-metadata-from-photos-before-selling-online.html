Remove metadata from photos before selling online

When you list an item from home, your photos can carry GPS coordinates and timestamps. Cleaning them first keeps your address and routine out of the listing files.

Why clean photos before you list something

Photos taken at home may include precise GPS coordinates. If a listing site or a buyer receives the original file, that location data could come with it. Some platforms strip metadata when you upload and others may not, and this can change over time, so it is safer to remove it yourself before the photo leaves your phone. This applies to marketplaces such as Facebook Marketplace, eBay, Craigslist or any site where you attach photos.

How to clean your listing photos

  1. Install ShareClean (free, for iPhone, iPad and Mac).
  2. Open ShareClean and select all the photos for your listing at once, or pick a photo in Photos, tap Share and choose ShareClean.
  3. ShareClean removes GPS coordinates, camera details, original timestamps and other metadata.
  4. Optionally resize to 2048px, 1024px or 512px, or convert to JPEG, to make uploads smaller. Use the live file size preview to check the result.
  5. Save the cleaned copies (by default to a ShareClean album) and upload those to your listing. Your originals are left unchanged.
Check the picture itself too. Metadata removal does not hide house numbers, license plates, mail, reflections or faces visible in the photo.

Questions

Do Marketplace or eBay remove photo location automatically?

This varies by platform and may change, and we cannot speak for any of them. Cleaning the photo yourself means the metadata is gone before the file is uploaded.

Can I clean many listing photos at once?

Yes. Open ShareClean and select several photos to clean them in one go.

Can I make the photos smaller too?

Yes. ShareClean can resize to 2048px, 1024px or 512px and adjust compression quality, with a live file size preview.

Does ShareClean upload my photos?

No. Everything is processed on your device. There are no accounts or servers.

Pricing. ShareClean is free. Available for iPhone, iPad and Mac (macOS 14 or later, Apple M1 chip or later).
