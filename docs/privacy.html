Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Overview

ShareClean is designed with privacy as its core principle. The app processes all photos entirely on your device. No data is collected, stored, transmitted, or shared with anyone.

Data Collection

ShareClean does not collect any data. Specifically:

  • No personal data is collected
  • No usage data is collected
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No crash reports are sent
  • No network requests are made by the app

Photo Processing

All photo processing — including metadata stripping, resizing, compression, and format conversion — happens locally on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to any server or transmitted over any network by the app.

Photo Library Access

ShareClean uses Apple's PhotosPicker API, which allows you to select specific photos without granting full library access. If you choose to use the "Scan My Library" feature, the app requests read access to your photo library solely to count photos containing GPS data. This scan happens on your device and no data leaves the app.

Third-Party Services

ShareClean does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

Data Storage

ShareClean stores only your preferences (such as output format, resize setting, and cleanup preset) locally on your device using UserDefaults. No personal information is stored.

Children's Privacy

ShareClean does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@waitedco.com.
