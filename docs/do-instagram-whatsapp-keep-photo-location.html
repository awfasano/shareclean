Do Instagram and WhatsApp keep photo location data?

Whether the photo you send still carries GPS data depends on the app, how you send it and the app version. Rather than guess, you can remove it before the photo leaves your phone.

It depends, so do not rely on it

Some apps strip location metadata when you post or send a photo, and others may keep it, especially when a photo is sent as a file or original. App behavior can change with updates, and we do not speak for any app. Even when an app removes metadata from the public copy, the original file you shared may still contain it.

What stays visible regardless

Apps may show a location tag you add yourself, and the picture's contents, such as landmarks or street signs, are unaffected by metadata removal.

Remove location before you share

  1. Install ShareClean (free, for iPhone, iPad and Mac).
  2. In Photos, select a photo and tap Share, then choose ShareClean.
  3. ShareClean removes GPS coordinates, camera details, timestamps and other metadata, and creates a cleaned copy.
  4. Choose the cleaned copy in Instagram, WhatsApp or any other app. In Messages you can also use ShareClean from the app drawer and tap Clean & Send.

Questions

Does Instagram remove location from photos?

We cannot say for certain, and behavior can change. Different apps handle metadata differently. Removing it yourself with ShareClean before you share avoids depending on that.

Does WhatsApp keep location data in photos?

It may depend on how you send the photo, for example as a regular photo or as a document or file. Cleaning the photo first means there is no location data to keep.

Yes. Use the Share Extension from Photos or other apps, or clean in the app and share the copy anywhere.

No. All processing happens on your device. There are no accounts, servers, advertising SDKs or analytics trackers.
